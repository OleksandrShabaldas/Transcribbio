Privacy policy

Effective 29 September 2026

Transcribbio is a personal, open-source project by Oleksandr Shabaldas. It runs entirely on your own devices. There is no Transcribbio server: the developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

Google Drive (optional “Cloud relay”)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Transcribbio requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata permission. It gives the app access to a single hidden application-data folder that belongs to Transcribbio in your own Google Drive. Transcribbio cannot see, read, change or delete any of your other Drive files.

  • What is stored there: lecture audio recordings made in the Transcribbio phone app, with their title, language and recording time — only while your computer can't be reached directly.
  • How it is used: the Transcribbio desktop app on your own computer downloads each recording to transcribe it, then deletes it from the folder. Nothing else reads it.
  • Account email: the app reads the email address of the connected Google account only to show which account is connected.

Other data

  • Recordings, transcripts and study materials are stored on your phone and your computer.
  • If you add your own Gemini API key, transcript text is sent to Google's Gemini API to correct it and generate study materials, under Google's terms. Without a key, that step is skipped or runs on a local model.
  • Transcribbio contains no analytics, advertising or tracking, and never sells or shares data.

Limited Use

Transcribbio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the relay feature described above, is not transferred to anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app's settings (phone and desktop).
  • Remove Transcribbio's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the hidden folder in Google Drive: Settings → Manage apps → Transcribbio → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Recordings on your devices can be deleted from within the apps.
