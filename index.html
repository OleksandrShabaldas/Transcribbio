Transcribbio

Lecture recording, transcription and study notes — on your own devices.

Transcribbio is a personal, open-source app. Record a lecture on your Android phone or Galaxy Watch; your Windows PC transcribes it locally with Whisper (Slovak and English), cleans it up, and turns it into summaries, notes, key takeaways and flashcards.

Recordings reach your PC over your home Wi-Fi, or — when that isn't possible — through a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive that the PC empties as soon as it has picked them up.
